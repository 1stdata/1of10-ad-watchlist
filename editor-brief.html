Edit instructions

Structure. Follow the beat table on the scripts page for each ad: the on-camera line, then the screen recording it calls for, then back to camera for the close. Target lengths: S1 about 50 s, S2 about 45 s, S5 about 40 s, S6 about 50 s. Hook on screen within the first second; no logo, no intro.

Captions. Burn in word-by-word captions, large, centered low third, white with a dark stroke. Keyword in each beat in the accent color (the number, the product name, "free").

Screen recordings (not in the folder, you record these inside the product). Each VE ad needs: the raw upload, AutoCut shrinking the timeline, Auto Retakes side by side, the color grade before/after, and the finished export. Each TG ad needs: connecting a channel, typing a title, the grid of six thumbnails appearing, one AI edit of text or background, and the download or send-to-YouTube step. For the S1 proof beats also capture the clock: a 12-minute video finishing in under 5 minutes, and six thumbnails in under 2 minutes, so the numbers on screen are true.

Numbers on screen. S1 VE proof: "12 min video, 5 min edit". S1 TG proof: "6 thumbnails, 2 minutes". S1 stakes beat: split frame, left "Time" with a clock, right "Money" with a price. S6 beat 2: cross out the expensive option (an editor invoice, a Photoshop icon).

End cards. Two seconds, URL 1of10.com and the CTA line Artur confirms for each product.

The S6 hook. Keep the walk continuous from the point at the lens through "Come follow me" to sitting at the desk, then cut to the locked-off body footage. Sound stab on "Hey you", quick "shh" or whisper SFX before the reveal line. Normal voice returns on the last line.

Audio. Lav audio is on the clips. Remove room tone between takes, light de-noise, then a music bed under everything at about -22 dB that drops out for the hook and the close.

Deliver v1 of S1 VE first. Artur reviews it against the beat table, then you run the rest the same way.
