Executive summary

Across 9 brands and 135 live Meta ads, the creative that survives past a year is not the prettiest. It is the ad with the clearest offer, a hook that names the viewer's problem in the first sentence, and a landing page built for one action. The three brands closest to 1of10's audience (vidIQ, OpusClip, Skool) all run the same play: a creator-voiced UGC video, a number in the first five seconds, one product feature demonstrated on screen, and a free trial or free clips as the ask.

The longest survivors are almost never single ads. OpusClip has one script running as 11 separate ads. Hormozi's workshop ad is live as three cuts of the same take. Skool keeps 15 different creator testimonials live at once, all pointing at the same signup URL. Longevity comes from the offer and the structure, then the brand spins many cuts on top of it.

Running duration is Foreplay's days-since-first-seen for ads still marked live in the Meta Ad Library. It is the best public proxy for a winner, not proof of one. Transcripts are Foreplay's rough auto speech-to-text.

Six patterns across the creator-tool brands

  1. The hook is a number or a named failure, inside 3 seconds. "64,000 per month with a skool about dog poop." "We're at three and a half million views." "I know why nobody's watching your YouTube videos." None open with the product name.
  2. One feature demonstrated on screen, not a feature list. The video shows the tool doing a single job; the primary text carries the emoji feature list.
  3. Creator-voiced UGC beats brand-voiced polish for tools. OpusClip's 11 and Skool's 15 are creators to camera. Adobe Video's music-only demos top out at 359 days versus 434 to 442 for the UGC brands.
  4. The offer is free entry, stated as a number. "25 free clips every month." "14-day free trial, then $9/month." "Free channel audit." CTA button stays Learn More; the headline carries the offer.
  5. Many cuts of one winner, not many concepts. One OpusClip script as 11 ads across two headlines, three durations and two destinations. Hormozi's workshop take as three cuts.
  6. One landing page per brand, repeated across every ad. skool.com/signup on all 15. opus.pro/try-opus-clip on 12 of 15. The page is fixed; creative rotates.
